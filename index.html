Merchant DQ Pipeline

A daily batch pipeline for marketplace orders, with data-quality checks at every layer. Nine of the 29 daily batches were damaged on purpose to prove the checks catch it. Everything on this page comes from the recorded run, not from a simulation.

The DAG

One Airflow run is one batch date. Runs go strictly in order because seller history and volume baselines depend on it, but a blocked day does not block the next one.
